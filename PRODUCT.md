# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors, customers, and app-store reviewers who need a reliable public reference for Pro Creations and its published applications, especially their governing legal documents.

## Product Purpose

Provide a concise public presence for Pro Creations and stable, discoverable policy URLs suitable for Google Play and Apple App Store listings.

## Brand Personality

Use the established site voice. Localization should translate the existing meaning without introducing a new personality or rewriting the brand.

## Anti-references

Avoid prominent localization controls, visual redesign, added marketing language, and localization that changes the substance of legal content.

## Design Principles

- Keep legal documents easy to find and navigate.
- Preserve meaning and structure across languages.
- Use stable, human-readable localized URLs.
- Keep language selection subtle and predictable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No project-specific target has been requested. Preserve semantic HTML, keyboard access, readable contrast, and correct document language metadata.
